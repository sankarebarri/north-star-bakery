About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to provide the community with fresh, handmade baked goods in a welcoming neighbourhood setting.

We believe a local bakery can be more than a place to buy bread. It can also be a place where neighbours, families, and local businesses feel connected.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We aim to select dependable, high-quality ingredients and use them carefully. Whenever practical, we value ingredients supplied by businesses and producers in our surrounding community.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers begin early each morning preparing breads, pastries, and special orders. Front-of-house team members help customers choose products and coordinate pre-orders.
